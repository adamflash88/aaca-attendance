# Teams reminders and staff access (flows)

Generated by `build_reminder_flows.py` / `build_staff_access_flow.py`, deployed by `Deploy-ReminderFlows.ps1` /
`Deploy-StaffAccessFlow.ps1` into the AACA Attendance solution (Dev; Test/Prod get them through the solution export).
Messages come from the **Flow bot** through the `aaca_sharedteams_reminders` connection reference (bind it to a
Microsoft Teams connection before turning the flows on). Times are Pacific.

**School day** (same rule as the app): a term at the campus covers the date, and it is a make-up day, or a weekday with
no closing calendar exception for that campus or all campuses. Reminders never go out on non-school days.

| Flow | When | Who | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| AACA - Teacher Attendance Reminder | Mon-Fri 10:00 | Each active teacher (Staff email, else linked account) with students not marked today | Count + names of the unmarked students |
| AACA - Office Daily Reminder | Mon-Fri 15:30 | Active Staff with App Role = Attendance Office at the campus (includes site administrators who have that role) | Attendance not marked (with teachers), absences to classify, parent/teacher conflicts, transportation not confirmed today, Mapping Errors for the campus. Nothing open = no message |
| AACA - Weekly Site Admin Summary | Fri 15:00 | Active Staff with Site Administrator = Yes at the campus | This week (Mon-today): school days, student-days not marked, student-days transport not confirmed; plus open absences to classify, conflicts, Mapping Errors |
| AACA - Staff Access Sync | every 15 min | - | Links active Staff rows with an Email and no User to the enabled account with that email; brand-new teachers (no roles yet) get the campus business unit, AACA Teacher + Basic User and the AACA Absence Viewers profile. Never changes existing users' access |

Definitions used in the counts:
- *Not marked*: enrolled at the campus that day (enrollment start <= day <= end) with no non-deleted Attendance row.
- *To classify*: absence (Present = No, not deleted) with no classification, dated 2026-01-01 (cut-over) through today.
- *Conflict*: Present = Yes and Parent Reported Absent = Yes (cleared by Keep present / Mark absent in Absences).
- *Transportation not confirmed*: active Student Transportation that day, student enrolled at the campus, and no Daily
  Transport row for that day.
- *Mapping Errors*: Student Absence Log items with Match_Status = "Needs Review" whose School Site is the campus.
